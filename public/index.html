how a day on VibeMatch goes

Three moments, not an endless scroll.

VibeMatch doesn't run on infinite matches. It runs on the actual rhythm of getting to know someone.

9:14 AM

morning prompt

One question drops daily — "narrate your coffee order like a sports commentator." Answer with a 20-second voice note. That's your profile for the day.

1:40 PM

the one match

VibeMatch sends a single match at lunch, picked from how you both answered — not how you both look. You get 48 hours to say something back.

6:20 PM

golden hour date

If it clicks, VibeMatch suggests a low-stakes first meetup nearby — coffee, a walk, a show — timed for right after work, not a whole evening's commitment.
